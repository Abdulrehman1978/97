# Research and Provenance Appendix

## 1. Problem Statement Context & PM-AJAY GIA Guidelines
**PM-AJAY (Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana)** is a merged central sector scheme aimed at the socio-economic empowerment of Scheduled Caste communities through three components:
1. Development of SC-dominated villages ("Adarsh Gram")
2. Grants-in-Aid for district-level multi-sectoral projects (GIA)
3. Construction of Hostels for SC students

### Core GIA Challenges Specifically Highlighted in SIH26097:
1. **Lack of Perspective Planning / Road Map**: Training allocations often occur without grounded assessment of actual community skills and localized market demands.
2. **Inadequate Field Support Teams**: Gram panchayat and block-level officers face overwhelming caseloads without automated intelligence, offline capability, or standardized playbooks.
3. **Absence of Trained Financial Consultants**: Enterprise pathways stall because beneficiaries lack guidance on bankability, working capital, and scheme subsidies (e.g. NSFDC, Stand-Up India, Mudra).
4. **Poor Post-Training Placement & Retention**: Many candidates complete certificate programs that have no local demand or fail to accommodate caregiving or mobility restrictions.
5. **Cross-Departmental Coordination Friction**: District skill committees, state corporations, training partners, and banks operate in information silos.

## 2. Competitor Baseline & Prior-Art Audit
Prior SIH26097 implementations demonstrated basic voice chatbots and course catalogues. However, an analysis of prior attempts revealed recurring structural flaws:
- **Flaw 1 (The Hallucination Trap)**: Using ungrounded LLMs as the final authority on scheme eligibility or course offerings, inventing batches and stipend promises.
- **Flaw 2 (Terminology Alienation)**: Expecting an unlettered rural youth to select "NCO 7231.0100" or understand "NSQF Level 4 Qualification Pack".
- **Flaw 3 (The Dead-End Recommendation)**: Showing an AI match percentage with no next steps, no human escalation, and no local batch availability truth state.
- **Flaw 4 (Neglecting Post-Skilling & Planning)**: Treating the beneficiary app as an isolated toy while ignoring district administrators and field workers.

### The LIP V3 Counter-Strategy:
- Separate language understanding (LLM/NLP extraction) from deterministic policy and eligibility verification.
- Feature the **Living Pathway**: turning spoken tasks and tools into an evidence-backed skill graph with transparent factor weights.
- Provide end-to-end operational portals for Field Workers, Employers, Training Providers, Financial Counsellors, and District Administrators.
