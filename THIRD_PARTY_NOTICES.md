# Third-Party Notices and Licensing Register

The Livelihood Intelligence Platform adheres to strict licensing, provenance, and attribution standards. No code, scraped datasets, or copyrighted artifacts from competitor repositories are copied directly. All government classifications and schemes are mapped from official public standards.

## 1. Classification & Standards Data
- **National Classification of Occupations (NCO-2015)**: Published by the Directorate General of Employment (DGE), Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India. Public open standard used for occupational taxonomy mapping.
- **National Qualifications Register (NQR) & NSQF**: Regulated by the National Council for Vocational Education and Training (NCVET), Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE). Public qualification specifications, QP codes, and competency standards.
- **Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana (PM-AJAY)**: Guidelines published by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE), Government of India. Used for eligibility rules, GIA components, and perspective planning frameworks.
- **One District One Product (ODOP)**: Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry. Used for district-level economic cluster signals.

## 2. Open-Source Software Components
- **FastAPI / Pydantic / SQLAlchemy / Alembic**: MIT / BSD Licensed modern Python asynchronous web and ORM foundation.
- **Next.js / React / TypeScript / Lucide-React / TailwindCSS**: MIT Licensed modern UI framework and design toolkit.
- **Web Speech API & Audio Standards**: W3C standard browser APIs for speech recognition and synthesis.
- **OpenStreetMap / MapLibre Standards**: Open data standards for geographic cartography.

## 3. Reused Competitor Code Policy
- Direct SIH26097 repositories (e.g. Saksham, Jeevika, Aarohan, KaushalSaathi) were reviewed purely as academic/prior-art benchmarks for problem-space understanding and edge-case discovery.
- **No competitor source code or UI assets have been copied.** All code is built clean-room from first principles to ensure 100% intellectual property clarity, security, and architectural integrity.
